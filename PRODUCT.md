# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Groups of friends who casually take turns covering small things for one another—drinks, coffees,
meals, tickets, and favors—and want an easy shared memory of whose turn comes next.

## Product Purpose

BeerMe makes informal, in-kind IOUs quick to record and easy for a whole group to understand.
Success means a friend can capture a moment in a few taps, everyone sees the same clear history,
and the group gets back to spending time together without awkward mental bookkeeping.

## Positioning

BeerMe remembers the little things friends return in kind without making friendship feel like
accounting.

## Brand Personality

Friendly, spirited, and trustworthy. The voice is conversational and concise, with the confidence
of a dependable friend and the lightness of the social moments the app supports.

## Anti-references

BeerMe should not feel like a bank, expense report, spreadsheet, crypto product, or enterprise
finance dashboard. It should avoid technical language, guilt-driven collection language, fake
social proof, and visual complexity that makes a five-second interaction feel like work.

## Design Principles

- Lead with the friendship, not the record keeping.
- Make the next useful action obvious and quick.
- Keep shared history honest and understandable.
- Use personality to make the experience warm, never to hide meaning.
- Explain the product through familiar moments rather than feature jargon.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast, provide logical landmarks and heading order, preserve keyboard and screen
reader usability, keep interactive targets at least 44 by 44 CSS pixels, and collapse motion for
people who prefer reduced motion. Beer is only the default: the language and interface must welcome
groups who track any item or favor they return in kind.
